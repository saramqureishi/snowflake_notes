# Behavioural & Game-Based Assessments — Deep Reference

These do not have "right answers" in the usual sense. They infer tendencies from how
you behave across many small tasks. Coaching here means understanding *what is being
measured* and performing as your genuine, best-focused self — not trying to fake a
profile (fakery is often detected via consistency checks, and misrepresenting
yourself backfires in the role).

## What they measure
- **Processing speed & accuracy trade-off** — how fast you work vs error rate.
- **Risk appetite** — willingness to gamble for higher reward (e.g. inflate-the-
  balloon style tasks).
- **Learning from feedback** — do you adjust strategy after errors?
- **Persistence & effort** — response to difficulty or repeated setbacks.
- **Attention & working memory** — digit spans, target detection.
- **Planning / impulsivity** — do you pause to plan, or act immediately?

## Provider notes
- **Arctic Shores**: gamified tasks producing trait metrics (e.g. resilience, risk,
  learning agility). Strategy to coach: steady low-error execution usually scores
  better than rushed guessing; adjust after feedback; stay consistent across tasks.
- **Cappfinity (strengths/SJT side)**: situational judgement and strengths framing —
  measures preferences and typical workplace behaviour. Coach the user to read the
  scenario's priorities (safety, customer, integrity, teamwork) and answer
  consistently with genuine strengths rather than second-guessing a "model answer".
- **Pymetrics-style**: neuroscience mini-games mapping effort, risk, fairness,
  attention to a trait profile matched against role benchmarks.

## How to coach (legitimately)
1. Explain the trait a given task type is probing.
2. Advise a consistent, honest strategy (e.g. "decide your accuracy-vs-speed stance
   and keep it steady").
3. Practise the *cognitive component* (memory spans, quick pattern spotting) with
   original drills — that's trainable.
4. Do **not** try to reverse-engineer a specific employer's scoring to fake a
   profile.

## Honest framing
There is no universal "best" profile — different roles weight traits differently.
The useful preparation is reducing surprise (knowing the formats), warming up
cognitive speed, and answering authentically.
