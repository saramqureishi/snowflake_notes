# Publisher Formats, Timings & Trap Profiles — Deep Reference

Formats vary by employer, role and version. The on-screen instructions in the real
test always take precedence. Use this to shape realistic **practice** drills.

## SHL (Verify / Interactive)
- **Formats**: data-interpretation tables & charts; 3×3 abstract matrices;
  deductive syllogisms; next-in-sequence; True/False/Cannot Say.
- **Timing**: often tight, ~45–75s per numerical/verbal item in Verify.
- **Trap profile**: footnotes and axis labels you skip; "percentage points"; option
  distractors built from the obvious wrong base.
- **Drill tip**: always include a footnote or unit twist in generated items.

## AON / cut-e
- **Formats**: high-speed micro-tasks — Switch Challenge, Scales, Digit Challenge,
  Chat/Grid inductive rule detection.
- **Timing**: very fast; many short items, adaptive.
- **Trap profile**: rule *switches* mid-stream; speed-induced slips.
- **Drill tip**: short items; inductive "find the switched rule" grids; scales solved
  with x + y = z.

## Cappfinity
- **Formats**: exact free-text numerical entry; numerical rank-ordering; multi-source
  table synthesis; situational judgement / strengths.
- **Timing**: moderate; exact entry means no option to reverse-engineer from.
- **Trap profile**: dynamic units (thousands vs millions, per-hour vs annual);
  rounding only at the final step.
- **Drill tip**: require an exact typed value; mix two source tables.

## Amberjack
- **Formats**: blended work-scenario items combining a passage + a data table + a
  prioritisation decision.
- **Trap profile**: mixing a direct fact with an unsupported assumption inside one
  scenario.
- **Drill tip**: give a short brief, a small table, and ask for the best-supported
  decision.

## HireVue (game-based & cognitive)
- **Formats**: spatial puzzles, digit-memory sequences, pattern matching, scenario
  decisions (plus separate video interviews — out of scope for reasoning drills).
- **Trap profile**: coordinate confusion in grids; memory decay in digit spans.
- **Drill tip**: coordinate-tracking grid tasks; forward/backward digit spans.

## Arctic Shores
- **Formats**: behavioural + cognitive gamified tasks → trait metrics.
- **Coaching stance**: optimise low-error cognitive strategy; stay consistent;
  there's no single "pass" answer. See `behavioural.md`.

## General timing discipline
- Budget time per item; if an item exceeds ~1.5× budget, make the best-supported
  choice and move on (in adaptive tests, abandoning accuracy for speed hurts more
  than one skipped item).
- Warm up before the real test with 5–10 original drills to prime recall.
